Shoulder Shrugs Exercise

Release shoulder tension and improve posture with simple shrugging movements

⏱️ Timer: 0:00

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Starting Position

Sit or stand with spine straight. Arms relaxed at your sides. Feet shoulder-width apart for stability.

2. Inhale and Lift

Take a deep breath in as you slowly lift both shoulders up toward your ears. Keep arms straight.

3. Hold at the Top

Hold the shrug at the top for 2-3 seconds. Feel the contraction in your trapezius muscles.

4. Exhale and Release

Slowly exhale as you release your shoulders down. Feel them drop below their starting position.

5. Repeat Sequence

Perform 10-15 repetitions. Focus on controlled movement rather than speed.

6. Add Variation (Optional)

Try shoulder circles: lift shoulders up, roll them back, then down and forward in circular motion.

💡 Breathing Pattern

Inhale: As you lift shoulders up

Exhale: As you release shoulders down

This breathing pattern enhances relaxation response

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Rushing through movements
  • Holding breath
  • Shrugging only one shoulder
  • Tensing neck muscles
  • Using momentum instead of muscle control
Progression Options
  • Hold light weights (1-2 lbs)
  • Increase hold time to 5 seconds
  • Add shoulder circles after shrugs
  • Combine with neck rolls

Benefits of Shoulder Shrugs

Physical Release

Releases tension in trapezius muscles, reduces shoulder stiffness

Posture Improvement

Strengthens upper back muscles, counteracts slouching

Stress Relief

Physical release triggers mental relaxation response
