Neck Rolls Exercise

Release tension in your neck and shoulders with gentle circular movements

⏱️ Timer: 0:00

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Starting Position

Sit or stand with your spine straight. Relax your shoulders down away from your ears. Place hands on your lap or by your sides.

2. Forward Movement

Gently drop your chin to your chest. Feel the stretch in the back of your neck. Hold for 2-3 breaths.

3. Right Ear to Shoulder

Slowly roll your head to the right, bringing your right ear toward your right shoulder. Don't lift the shoulder.

4. Backward Movement

Continue rolling your head back, looking up at the ceiling. Don't force it if uncomfortable. Keep jaw relaxed.

5. Left Ear to Shoulder

Complete the circle by rolling your head to the left side. Move slowly and smoothly.

6. Repeat & Reverse

Perform 5 slow rolls clockwise, then 5 counter-clockwise. Focus on smooth, continuous motion.

💡 Important Safety Tips
  • Move slowly - never jerk or force the movement
  • Keep shoulders relaxed and down
  • Breathe deeply throughout the movement
  • Stop immediately if you feel any sharp pain
  • If you have neck injuries, consult a doctor first

Benefits of Neck Rolls

Physical Benefits
  • Releases neck and shoulder tension
  • Improves cervical spine mobility
  • Reduces tension headache frequency
  • Improves posture alignment
  • Increases blood flow to the head
Mental & Emotional Benefits
  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Improves mental clarity and focus
  • Promotes mindfulness and presence
  • Enhances mind-body connection
  • Creates sense of physical release
