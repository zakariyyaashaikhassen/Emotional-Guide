  • Breathe naturally

Legs Up the Wall

  • Deep relaxation

Benefits of Movement

Physical Benefits:
  • Releases muscle tension
  • Improves circulation
  • Reduces pain and stiffness
  • Boosts energy levels
  • Improves posture
Mental/Emotional Benefits:
  • Reduces stress hormones
  • Releases endorphins
  • Improves mood
  • Clears mental fog
  • Enhances focus
Daily Movement Goal:
Today's movement: 0/3 sessions
Quick Movement Ideas
  • Stand up every 30 minutes
  • 5-minute walk after meals
  • Stretch during phone calls
  • Park farther away
  • Take stairs instead of elevator
  • Dance to one song
  • Gentle yoga before bed

Tip: Even 2 minutes of movement can reset your nervous system.
