Legs Up the Wall

Deep relaxation pose to calm nervous system and reduce swelling

Getting Started

This restorative pose helps calm the nervous system, reduce swelling in legs and feet, and promote deep relaxation. Practice for 5-15 minutes.

How to Set Up Legs Up the Wall

1. Find Your Space

Choose a clear wall space. Remove any obstacles. Have a yoga mat or blanket for comfort.

2. Position Yourself

Sit with your right side against the wall, knees bent. Your hip should be as close to the wall as comfortable.

3. Swing Legs Up

As you lie back, gently swing your legs up the wall. Use your hands for support if needed.

4. Adjust Position

Your sitting bones don't need to touch the wall. Find a comfortable distance (6-12 inches away is fine).

5. Relax Arms

Place arms at your sides, palms facing up. This open position helps with relaxation.

6. Close Eyes & Breathe

Close your eyes. Begin deep, slow breathing. Let gravity do the work - no muscle effort needed.

Comfort Modifications
  • Support under hips: Folded blanket for elevation
  • Knee bend: Bend knees slightly if hamstrings are tight
  • Arm position: Hands on belly or overhead
  • Eye cover: Light cloth over eyes for deeper relaxation
Getting Out Safely
  1. Bend knees and roll to right side
  2. Rest in fetal position for 30 seconds
  3. Use hands to push up to seated
  4. Sit quietly for a minute before standing
Contraindications & Precautions
  • Avoid if: You have glaucoma or serious eye issues
  • Use caution if: You have high blood pressure (consult doctor)
  • Modify if: You're pregnant (use pillows for support)
  • Stop if: You feel dizziness or discomfort

Benefits of Legs Up the Wall

Circulatory Benefits
  • Reduces leg and foot swelling
  • Improves venous return
  • Decreases varicose vein discomfort
  • Lowers heart rate
Nervous System Benefits
  • Activates parasympathetic system
  • Reduces cortisol (stress hormone)
  • Increases heart rate variability
  • Promotes deep relaxation
Musculoskeletal Benefits
  • Gently stretches hamstrings
  • Releases lower back tension
  • Reduces spinal compression
  • Relieves tired leg muscles
Mental/Emotional Benefits
  • Reduces anxiety symptoms
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Calms racing thoughts
  • Enhances mood regulation

Enhance Your Experience
  • Dim the lights or use eye mask
  • Play gentle instrumental music
  • Use essential oils (lavender)
  • Place pillow under head/neck
  • Wear comfortable, loose clothing
  • Keep room slightly cool
Mindfulness Prompts

While in the pose, focus on:

  • Sensation of gravity on legs
  • Rhythm of your breath
  • Release in lower back
  • Feeling of support from floor
  • Any pulsing or tingling in legs
