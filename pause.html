Pause Techniques

Learn to pause before reacting and respond thoughtfully instead of impulsively.
⏸️

The 4-Step Pause Protocol

Respond, don't react

1
Notice the Trigger

Recognize what triggered your emotional reaction. Name it without judgment.

2
Take 3 Deep Breaths

Inhale deeply, exhale slowly. This creates space between trigger and response.

3
Ask Yourself

"What do I need right now?" "What response would serve me best?"

4
Choose Your Response

Select a thoughtful, intentional response instead of an automatic reaction.

Quick Pause Tools

10-Second Pause

Count slowly from 10 to 1 before responding.

Physical Pause

Change your physical position before reacting.

Water Break Pause

Take a sip of water before responding.

Walk Away Pause

Excuse yourself for 2 minutes to reset.

Common Triggers & Pause Strategies

Trigger Impulsive Reaction Pause Strategy Thoughtful Response
Criticism Defensiveness Breathe 3 times "Help me understand..."
Stress overload Snapping at others Excuse for 5 min "I need a moment"
Feeling rushed Panic decisions Count to 10 slowly "Let me think about this"
Unexpected change Resistance/frustration Name 3 things you see "I can adapt to this"
Feeling attacked Counter-attack Sip of water "I hear you're upset"

Practice Pausing

Daily Pause Challenge:

Practice the 4-step pause protocol once today.

Pause Reminders:
  • Set phone reminders to pause
  • Place sticky notes as cues
  • Practice during low-stress times
Benefits of Pausing
  • Reduces regretful reactions
  • Improves relationships
  • Lowers stress levels
  • Increases self-awareness
  • Enhances decision quality
  • Builds emotional intelligence

Remember: The space between stimulus and response is where your power lies.
