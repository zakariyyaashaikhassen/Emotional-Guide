Wrist Circles Exercise

Improve wrist mobility and prevent strain from typing and device use

⏱️ Timer: 0:00

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Starting Position

Sit or stand comfortably. Extend your right arm forward at shoulder height, palm facing down.

2. Make a Gentle Fist

Make a soft fist (not tight) with your right hand. Keep fingers relaxed, not clenched.

3. Clockwise Circles

Slowly rotate your wrist clockwise, making small circles. Start with 10 circles.

4. Counter-Clockwise Circles

Reverse direction, making 10 counter-clockwise circles. Keep movements smooth.

5. Switch Hands

Repeat the same sequence with your left hand: 10 clockwise, 10 counter-clockwise.

6. Finger Stretch (Optional)

After circles, spread fingers wide, hold for 5 seconds, then make a gentle fist.

Variation 1: Extended Arms

Extend both arms forward simultaneously, circling wrists together. Improves coordination.

Variation 2: Prayer Position

Palms together in prayer position, rotate wrists while keeping palms together.

⚠️ Important Precautions
  • Stop if you feel sharp pain (mild stretching is normal)
  • If you have carpal tunnel syndrome, consult doctor first
  • Move slowly, don't jerk the wrists
  • Keep shoulders relaxed during exercise

Benefits for Computer Users

Prevents Strain

Reduces risk of repetitive strain injury (RSI) from typing

Improves Circulation

Increases blood flow to hands and fingers

Reduces Stiffness

Maintains wrist flexibility and range of motion

When to Practice
  • Every 30 minutes of computer use
  • Before starting typing work
  • After long phone use
  • When hands feel stiff or cold
  • During video calls (off-camera)
  • While waiting for programs to load

Ideal frequency: 5-10 times daily for computer users
