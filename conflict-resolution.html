🤝 Conflict Resolution

Turning disagreements into opportunities for growth

Healthy vs. Unhealthy Conflict

Conflict is normal in relationships. The difference between healthy and unhealthy conflict is how you handle it.

Healthy Conflict
  • Focus on the issue, not the person
  • Listening to understand
  • Respectful communication
  • Working toward solution
  • Leads to growth
Unhealthy Conflict
  • Personal attacks and blame
  • Yelling or silent treatment
  • Bringing up past issues
  • Winning at all costs
  • Damages relationship

🔄 The 4-Step Resolution Process

1
Pause & Calm

Take a break if emotions are high

2
Listen & Understand

Hear each other's perspectives

3
Express & Problem-Solve

Find mutually agreeable solutions

4
Agree & Move Forward

Make a plan and let go of resentment

💬 Phrases That Help Resolve Conflict

To De-escalate
  • "Let's take a 10-minute break"
  • "I want to understand your point"
  • "This is important to me"
  • "Can we start over?"
To Find Solutions
  • "What would work for both of us?"
  • "How can we prevent this next time?"
  • "Let's find a compromise"
  • "What's most important here?"
