Physical Grounding

Connect with your body

Use physical sensations to anchor yourself in the present moment.

Pressure & Touch
  • Press feet firmly into floor
  • Clench and release fists
  • Hold a cold/warm object
  • Touch different textures
Movement
  • Stretch arms and legs
  • Roll shoulders slowly
  • Stand up and sit down
  • Walk barefoot
🧠

Mental Grounding

Focus your mind

Use cognitive exercises to redirect your thoughts away from anxiety.

Counting & Lists
  • Count backwards from 100 by 7
  • List all 50 states
  • Name animals A-Z
  • Recite multiplication tables
Descriptions
  • Describe your environment
  • Recite a poem or song
  • Plan a detailed meal
  • List your favorite movies

When to Use Grounding

Grounding techniques are especially helpful when you feel:

  • Anxious or panicky
  • Overwhelmed by emotions
  • Disconnected from reality
  • Stuck in negative thoughts
  • Experiencing flashbacks
Tips for Effective Grounding:
  • Practice regularly, not just in crisis
  • Engage multiple senses at once
  • Be specific in your observations
  • Combine with deep breathing
  • Notice without judgment
