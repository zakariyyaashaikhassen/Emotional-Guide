Create Your Own

Personalized affirmations work best

Tips for effective affirmations:
  • Use present tense ("I am" not "I will be")
  • Make them believable and realistic
  • Keep them positive and specific
  • Repeat them daily with feeling

Affirmations by Category

Self-Worth
  • I am enough just as I am.
  • I deserve love and respect.
  • My needs and feelings matter.
  • I am worthy of good things.
Anxiety & Calm
  • I am safe in this moment.
  • I can handle whatever comes.
  • I choose peace over worry.
  • I release what I cannot control.
Strength & Resilience
  • I am stronger than my struggles.
  • Challenges help me grow.
  • I have overcome before.
  • I am resilient and adaptable.
Self-Care & Rest
  • I give myself permission to rest.
  • Taking care of me is important.
  • My well-being matters.
  • I honor my limits and boundaries.
