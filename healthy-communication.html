🗣️ Healthy Communication

Learn to express yourself clearly and listen effectively

The Foundation of Good Relationships

Healthy communication is about expressing yourself honestly while also respecting and listening to others. It's a skill that can be learned and improved with practice.

Speaking Clearly
  • Use "I" statements
  • Be specific about needs
  • Stay calm and respectful
  • Choose the right time
Active Listening
  • Give full attention
  • Don't interrupt
  • Ask clarifying questions
  • Repeat back what you heard

🔁 Communication Do's and Don'ts

✅ DO
  • "I feel frustrated when plans change last minute"
  • "Can we talk about this when we're both calm?"
  • "What I hear you saying is..."
  • "I need some quiet time to think"
❌ DON'T
  • "You always ruin our plans!"
  • "You never listen to me!"
  • "That's a stupid idea"
  • Give silent treatment
