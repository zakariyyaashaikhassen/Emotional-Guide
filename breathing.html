Diaphragmatic Breathing

Belly Breathing for Deep Relaxation

Breathe deeply into your belly, not your chest. This engages the diaphragm fully and is the foundation for all breathing exercises.

  1. Place one hand on chest, one on belly
  2. Inhale slowly through nose, feel belly rise
  3. Exhale slowly through pursed lips
  4. Keep chest relatively still
Focus on your belly rising and falling

Why Breathing Works

Deep breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system ("rest and digest"), reducing cortisol and calming the mind.

Tips for Success:
  • Practice daily for 5 minutes
  • Use before stressful events
  • Combine with gentle movement
  • Focus on exhale being longer than inhale
  • Start with 3-5 rounds, build gradually

🧠 The Science of Breath

When you breathe deeply:

  • Vagus nerve is stimulated, slowing heart rate
  • Cortisol levels drop significantly
  • Oxygen exchange improves in lungs
  • Blood pressure normalizes
  • Brain waves shift to alpha (relaxed) state

Just 5 minutes of deep breathing can reduce anxiety by up to 40%.

⏰ When to Practice

  • Morning: Set calm tone for day
  • Before meals: Improve digestion
  • During breaks: Reset your nervous system
  • Before bed: Prepare for restful sleep
  • When stressed: Immediate calm response
